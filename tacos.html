Classic Beef Tacos

Description

Flavorful seasoned ground beef served inside warm tortillas and topped with crisp lettuce, fresh tomatoes, shredded cheese, and salsa.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 1 tablespoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 8 taco shells or small flour tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 medium tomato, diced

Steps

  1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat and cook the ground beef until browned, breaking it apart with a spoon.
  2. Drain any excess grease from the skillet.
  3. Stir in the chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and water.
  4. Simmer uncovered for 5 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats the beef.
  5. Warm the taco shells or tortillas according to package directions.
  6. Fill each shell with seasoned beef and top with lettuce, diced tomatoes, and shredded cheddar cheese before serving.
